  - paragraph: Depending on where and when you park in Chicago, the cost and hours of operation vary. We operate 365 days/year, unless otherwise posted. Please see the rate map below for more information.
  - generic: PRICE RANGE
  - paragraph: Whether you use ParkChicago® or the pay boxes on the street, hourly parking rates range from $2.50 (outside the Loop and Central Business District) to $4.75 (Central Business District), to $4.75 (West Loop) and $7.00 (Loop).
  - generic: CHECK SIGNAGE
  - paragraph: Always remember to check the on-street pay boxes, ParkChicago®, or ParkChicago® Map for specific rates and times for where you parked, along with checking on-street signage posted by the City. The pay boxes and ParkChicago® will not accept payment if a parking fee is not required.

    - paragraph: In the Central Business District outside the Loop (area bounded by Lake Michigan to the east, North Avenue to the north, Halsted to the west, and Roosevelt Road to the south), rates are $4.75 per hour from 8:00 a.m. to midnight.

    - strong: "Pro Tip:"
    - strong: ZoneHop...Move Your Time
    - text: Your purchased time can be moved and good until expiration at most meters OF THE SAME TYPE OF SPACE AND WITH THE SAME OR LOWER HOURLY RATE. However, purchased time cannot be moved between on-street meters and off-street metered lots, OR BETWEEN COMMERCIAL LOADING ZONES AND OTHER TYPES OF ON-STREET METERED PARKING SPACES. If you do move your vehicle to a space where moving time is permitted, please be sure to abide by all zone signage regarding restrictions and rules, including all signage of which may be posted on the SAME SIDE OF THE STREET ON THE BLOCK you parked your vehicle. For example, you can move your vehicle from a $4.75 zone to another $4.75 zone or $2.50 zone. If the rate at the new location is higher, you must create a new parking session or pay at the pay box. Make sure to check for rates, parking restrictions on the pay box and permanent or temporary signage to avoid a ticket.
